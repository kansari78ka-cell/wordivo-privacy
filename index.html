Privacy Policy for WORDIVO

Last updated: October 14, 2026

The koolk's app ("we", "us", "our") provides the mobile game WORDIVO (the "App"). This policy explains what information the App handles and what choices you have. By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We do not require you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or precise location.

Game data stored on your device

The App saves your game progress (coins, completed levels, scores, stars, daily-reward streak, language and sound settings) locally on your device. This data is not sent to us or to any server. It is deleted when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

Information collected automatically by advertising services

The App shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob and Google may collect and use certain information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address (and approximate location derived from it), device and operating-system details, app-interaction and ad-interaction data, and crash/diagnostic data. This is used to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and keep the service secure. We do not receive your personal identity from this process.

2. How we use information

  • To run the game and save your progress on your device.
  • To show ads that help keep the App free.
  • To keep the App stable and secure.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Third-party services

The App uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK. Google's use of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

4. Ads and your choices

  • Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and some other regions), the App asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice from the Settings screen of the App, where that option is shown.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalization, in your Android device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads).
  • If you decline personalization, you may still see ads, but they will not be personalized.

5. Children's privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us at kansari78.ka@gmail.com and we will help resolve it.

Because we do not collect your personal data on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete. All game data on your device can be removed by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App. For data held by Google through advertising services, please use your Google account's data controls.
